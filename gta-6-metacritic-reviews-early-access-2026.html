How Are Players Playing GTA 6 Early via New Zealand?

Because digital console storefronts unlock rolling midnight releases based on regional account settings, players in the UK and Europe frequently look into the New Zealand timezone trick:

  • Xbox Series X|S Players: Changing the console's system location to New Zealand in Settings allows digital pre-order owners to launch the game as soon as New Zealand hits midnight local time—hours ahead of UK GMT midnight.
  • PlayStation 5 Players: Unlike Xbox, PS5 unlock timers are tied directly to the region of the PlayStation Network account used to purchase the game, meaning a dedicated New Zealand PSN store purchase is required for earlier regional access.

When Is GTA 6 Online Coming Out?

Following the same staggered launch model Rockstar utilized for previous blockbusters, GTA 6 Online is not active on Day One of the single-player story release. Rockstar has designed a brief initial window so players can experience Lucia and Jason's 50-to-60-hour campaign before multiplayer servers go live globally within the coming weeks, ensuring smooth server stability at launch.
